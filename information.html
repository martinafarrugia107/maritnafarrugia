Martina Farrugia (1998) is a multidisciplinary artist and designer based between Malta and the Netherlands. With a background in art history, archaeology and digital art, her current artistic practice explores tangible cultural heritage and collective memory, translating her research and findings through various media. She has recently graduated from the Masters in Experimental Publishing at the Piet Zwart Institute, focusing on merging the material worlds of crafts and communication technology.

Her work has been exhibited in group exhibitions across the Netherlands, Malta, and Romania, including MaMA (Rotterdam), Embassies of Design – Design Passage (The Hague), the Malta Society of Arts, and Romanian Creative Week (2025).
