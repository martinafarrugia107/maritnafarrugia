Anything not saved will be lost

During the exhibition Modding The Mirror World by Louisa Teichmann, I hosted Anything Not Saved Will Be Lost, an interactive treasure hunt within the MaMA showroom. Participants explored the exhibition using an online manual featuring photogrammetry-based images and videos of ceramic objects hidden throughout the space. Inspired by discarded household items found on Rotterdam’s streets, each object was accompanied by a narrative clue and could be claimed and taken home once found. The event explored themes of archiving, memory, ownership, and the value we assign to everyday objects, inviting participants to engage with both the exhibition and the city through acts of discovery and preservation.
